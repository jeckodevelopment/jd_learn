Email notification queue

The queue reports messages processed by the scheduled delivery system. A failed entry must not automatically be considered undelivered: transport failures can leave delivery uncertain.

Check the scheduled task, the matching Joomla Mail Template for the event and language, canonical placeholders and permissions. Controlled retry is allowed only when the screen validates its preconditions, such as a template validation failure before SMTP transport.

Do not edit queue states directly in the database, duplicate deliveries or disclose addresses and contents in troubleshooting reports.

Read the full procedure in the administrator manual: Email notification queue
