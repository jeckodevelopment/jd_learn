Configuration and tools

The administration dashboard provides shortcuts allowed by your role and provider/email-task status. A diagnostic indicator does not guarantee playback in every browser.

For video, check installed and enabled plugins, the source, privacy consent, embedding policy and credentials where applicable. Site settings can still prevent playback even when the provider looks configured.

For communications, verify that Joomla Mail Templates and the scheduled task are present and valid. Do not expose tokens, secrets or learner data during troubleshooting.
