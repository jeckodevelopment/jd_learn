Dashboard and configuration

  1. Review the shortcuts available to your role and the status of video providers and mail tasks.
  2. For provider issues, verify the enabled plugin, configuration, consent and embedding policy.
  3. Inspect scheduled tasks in Joomla before assuming that queued email is being delivered.

If a command is unavailable, check ACL permissions, version and item state before retrying. Do not edit the database or historical documents directly.
