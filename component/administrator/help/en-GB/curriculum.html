Curriculum and lessons

Edit modules and lessons in the course working version. Save your edits and run publication preflight: saving a lesson does not publish the version.

Video providers depend on installed plugins, privacy consent and permissions from external services. For YouTube and Vimeo, confirm embedding is allowed; for Cloudflare Stream and HTML5, check provider configuration and the availability of the resource. An incomplete draft must not be published without validation.

Changes to a published curriculum do not automatically replace the version assigned to learners. Check blockers, completion rules and learner impact before changing publication state.
