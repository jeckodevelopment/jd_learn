Certificates list

This view lists certificates and administrator diagnostics for completions that may not have produced a document. Course completion does not always imply issuance: published-version rules, enabled configuration and a valid template are required.

For a missing certificate, inspect the diagnostic conditions. Do not force retroactive issuance or alter historical versions without investigating the case.

Details and downloads are restricted to authorized roles. Check PDFs with test data and do not share personal documents.
