Course communications

Prepare messages for course learners according to permissions and consent preferences. Preview and test delivery are not the same as sending the campaign to learners.

Check intended recipients, content and template, any attachments and the publication window. Queueing is separate from actual delivery by the Joomla task; avoid duplicate submits and verify status in the Email queue.

Do not include other learners' personal data in generic messages. Send tests only to authorized recipients.

Read the full procedure in the administrator manual: Course communications
